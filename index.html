Executive Background

Bridging Technical CRM Architecture with Enterprise ARR Growth

Technology alone does not generate revenue — connected operations do. Over the past 11+ years, I have helped enterprise B2B organizations transform fragmented marketing stacks into predictable revenue engines.

My journey began in Sales Operations at KONE, where I spent over six years configuring Salesforce security models, user permissions, and sales reporting dashboards for 100,000+ prospect records. Working closely with sales leaders taught me an invaluable lesson: marketing systems are only as valuable as the sales pipeline they generate.

Transitioning into Marketing Operations and senior consulting roles at RPM International (Carboline), HCLTech, and Temenos, I led complex end-to-end implementations of Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) and HubSpot across global business units. I architected multi-touch attribution frameworks, UTM tracking standards, and dynamic lead scoring models that eliminated sales follow-up lag and boosted MQL-to-SQL conversion by 30%.

"I do not view Pardot or Salesforce as standalone software tools. I architect them as the core nervous system of enterprise Revenue Operations — aligning Marketing, Sales, Data, and AI to scale predictable ARR."

Today, as a MarTech Architect & Salesforce Consultant, I partner with enterprise executive teams to eliminate revenue leakage, streamline lead handoffs across regions, integrate cutting-edge data enrichment agents like Clay, and build bulletproof RevOps systems built for sustainable growth.
